# Regulatory, Platform & Clinical-Safety Landscape — AI Anxiety Companion (as of Sept 2026)

Research memo compiled 13 September 2026 from web sources; every claim carries a link. Summarised in `../01-product-deep-dive.md` section 5 and applied in `../03-30-day-launch-plan.md` Phase 2.

## 1. United States

### 1.1 FDA: wellness vs. SaMD
- FDA republished its **General Wellness: Policy for Low Risk Devices** guidance on **6 Jan 2026** (with a Feb 2026 town hall). Wellness products must be non-invasive, low-risk, and intended to "support or promote a healthy lifestyle" — not to diagnose, treat, mitigate or prevent a specific disease/condition. Claims referencing specific diseases, diagnostic thresholds, or that "prompt or guide specific clinical action" fall outside. The revision focuses on physiologic-sensor wearables and does **not** directly address AI/chatbots. ([Covington](https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/01/fda-issues-revised-guidance-on-general-wellness-products), [Faegre Drinker](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/1/key-updates-in-fdas-2026-general-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-guidance), [FDA town hall](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-devices-news-and-events/town-hall-general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices-final-guidance-02112026))
- Practical line: "stress management / relaxation / building coping skills" = wellness; "treats/reduces symptoms of generalized anxiety disorder / panic disorder" = device claim. Software functions intended solely for maintaining a healthy lifestyle unrelated to disease are excluded from the device definition (21st Century Cures Act §3060). ([Troutman](https://www.troutman.com/insights/fdas-2026-guidance-on-general-wellness-devices-policy-for-low-risk-devices/))
- **Digital Health Advisory Committee, 6 Nov 2025** (second-ever meeting) on generative-AI mental-health devices, using a hypothetical Rx LLM therapy chatbot for MDD. Outcomes: committee wanted rigorous premarket evidence of "clinically significant outcomes under real-world conditions", differentiated adjunctive vs. standalone and Rx vs. OTC; flagged model drift and questioned how far PCCPs can go; called for "reliable mechanisms to detect and escalate acute safety concerns, such as suicidal ideation… to ensure timely human intervention"; stressed continued clinician oversight; labeling must disclose automatic modifications. Notably, low-risk "anxiety-skill builders" may fall under enforcement discretion and general-wellness apps sit outside the device definition. Docket closed 8 Dec 2025; no final policy yet. ([FDA meeting page](https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar/november-6-2025-digital-health-advisory-committee-meeting-announcement-11062025), [Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2025/12/the-mind-the-machine-and-the-model-drift-fdas), [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/fdas-digital-health-advisory-committee-weighs-guardrails-for-generative-ai-in-mental-health-devices), [Sidley](https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2025/11/us-fda-and-cms-actions-on-generative-ai-enabled-mental-health-devices-yield-insights-across-ai))
- Cautionary precedent: Woebot shut its DTC app on 30 Jun 2025, citing the cost of FDA authorization and inability to use LLMs under the current framework. ([STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2025/07/02/woebot-therapy-chatbot-shuts-down-founder-says-ai-moving-faster-than-regulators/))

### 1.2 FTC
- 11 Sep 2025: FTC issued **Section 6(b) orders** to seven companion-chatbot operators (Alphabet, Character.AI, Meta/Instagram, OpenAI, Snap, xAI) on safety testing, monetization, engagement design and minors. Not enforcement, but a signal. ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2025/09/ftc-ai-chatbots), [APA](https://www.apaservices.org/advocacy/news/federal-trade-commission-chatbot-companions))
- Health-data enforcement against non-HIPAA mental-health apps is established: **BetterHelp** ($7.8M, sharing anxiety/depression data with ad platforms) and **Cerebral** ($7M). The amended **Health Breach Notification Rule** treats unauthorized sharing with third parties (e.g., ad SDKs) as a "breach." ([Frier Levitt](https://www.frierlevitt.com/articles/ftc-orders-virtual-mental-health-platform-to-pay-7-8-million-to-resolve-data-disclosure-allegations/), [McDermott on HBNR](https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/ftc-amends-health-breach-notification-rule-to-regulate-health-apps-and-expand-breach-notification-requirements/))

### 1.4 HIPAA vs. state consumer-health-data laws
- A DTC wellness app that doesn't bill insurers or act for a covered entity is generally **not** a HIPAA covered entity/BA; its data falls to FTC Act §5, the HBNR and state law. ([A&O Shearman](https://www.aoshearman.com/en/insights/my-health-my-data-act-targets-entities-not-subject-to-hipaa-and-provides-a-private-right-of-action))
- **Washington My Health My Data Act** (in force since Mar 2024) covers "consumer health data" incl. mental-health status inferred from app inputs; requires a separate consumer-health privacy policy, opt-in consent for collection/sharing beyond what's necessary, **signed authorization for any sale**, geofencing ban, and a **private right of action**. Nevada SB 370 and Connecticut have similar regimes. ([RCW 19.373](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.373&full=true), [Stoel Rives FAQ](https://www.stoel.com/insights/publications/faq-washington-states-my-health-my-data-act))

## 2. United Kingdom
- **MHRA "Digital mental health technology: qualification and classification"** (Feb 2025, Wellcome-funded): qualification turns on intended purpose + functionality; tools that claim to treat/alleviate a mental disorder or influence diagnosis/treatment are SaMD (UKCA). A 27 Jan 2026 public-facing guide with NHS England followed; an AI-specific framework informed by AI Airlock is due in 2026. ([gov.uk collection](https://gov.uk/government/collections/digital-mental-health-technology), [Bristows](https://inquisitiveminds.bristows.com/post/102jzqs/a-wellcome-development-for-samd-the-uk-publishes-guidance-on-digital-mental-hea), [EMHIC](https://emhicglobal.com/global-news/mhra-issues-new-guidance-for-people-using-mental-health-apps-and-technologies/))
- **NHS DTAC** refreshed form mandatory from **6 Apr 2026** (25% fewer questions; still five domains: clinical safety [DCB0129/0160 + Clinical Safety Officer], data protection, technical security incl. Software Security Code of Practice and MFA, interoperability, usability/accessibility). Only needed if you sell into the NHS. ([Burges Salmon](https://www.burges-salmon.com/articles/102mnjh/new-nhs-digital-technology-assessment-criteria-what-health-tech-suppliers-need-t/), [NHS ADRS](https://digitalregulations.innovation.nhs.uk/regulations-and-guidance-for-developers/all-developers-guidance/using-the-digital-technology-assessment-criteria-dtac/))
- **ICO**: mental-health inputs are special-category "data concerning health"; need Art. 6 basis + Art. 9 condition (explicit consent for DTC), a DPIA (new tech + special-category + monitoring), and appropriate-policy documentation. ([ICO special category](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/))
- UK crisis routes: Samaritans **116 123** (free, 24/7), Shout — text **SHOUT to 85258**, NHS **111 option 2** for urgent mental-health support, 999 if life at risk. ([Samaritans](https://www.samaritans.org/how-we-can-help/contact-samaritan/talk-us-phone/), [Shout](https://giveusashout.org/get-help/), [NHS England](https://www.england.nhs.uk/2024/08/nhs-111-offering-crisis-mental-health-support-for-the-first-time/))

## 3. European Union
- **AI Act risk tier**: a consumer wellness companion without medical-device status is generally **not** Annex III high-risk; it becomes high-risk if it is an MDR device (Annex I) or provides clinical judgment/triage for essential services. Article 5 prohibitions (in force since 2 Feb 2025) matter: Commission guidelines give the example of a "therapeutic chatbot" for people with mental disabilities nudging them toward harmful behaviour or purchases as **prohibited** exploitation of vulnerability. ([Tandem Health](https://tandemhealth.ai/resources/knowledge/ai-act-risk-classification-mental-health-tools), [Commission prohibited-practices guidelines](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-08/guidelines_on_prohibited_artificial_intelligence_practices_established_by_regulation_eu_20241689_ai_act_english_ied3r5nwo50xggpcfmwckm3nuc_112367-1.PDF), [FPF](https://fpf.org/blog/red-lines-under-the-eu-ai-act-understanding-manipulative-techniques-and-the-exploitation-of-vulnerabilities/))
- **Article 50 transparency applied on 2 Aug 2026** and was *not* deferred by the Digital Omnibus (which pushed Annex III high-risk to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I to Aug 2028). Art. 50(1): users must be informed they're interacting with AI; Art. 50(3) emotion-recognition disclosure applies only to **biometric-based** inference — text-based mood inference is out of scope, but the Commission guidelines say keystroke/behavioural biometrics are in. Fines up to €15M/3%. ([CSA research note](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-20260729/), [Commission FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act), [Oliver Patel on emotion recognition](https://oliverpatel.substack.com/p/emotion-recognition-and-the-eu-ai))
- **GDPR Art. 9**: anxiety logs and pattern-learning are health data; explicit, specific consent (no bundling with T&Cs), DPIA, data minimisation, and the preventive-medicine exception is not available to non-clinical apps. ([GDPRhub Art 9](https://gdprhub.eu/Article_9_GDPR), [Momentum](https://www.themomentum.ai/blog/gdpr-consent-requirements-health-data))
- **MDR boundary**: MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1 (Jun 2025) — software for "lifestyle or wellness purposes" is not MDSW; qualification is by *intended purpose* (claims), not capability. ([MDCG 2019-11](https://health.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2020-09/md_mdcg_2019_11_guidance_en_0.pdf), [Emergo on Rev.1](https://www.emergobyul.com/news/european-revision-primary-software-guidance-mdcg-2019-11-revision-1-small-changes-meaningful))

## 4. Hong Kong & Singapore
- **HK PDPO**: no "special category" regime, but PCPD's **AI: Model Personal Data Protection Framework** (Jun 2024) expects AI governance, risk assessment, minimisation, human oversight proportional to risk, and transparency; a Mar 2025 checklist covers staff GenAI use; DPO's **GenAI Technical and Application Guideline** (Apr 2025) proposes a 4-tier risk model. PCPD's mobile-app best-practice guide applies (PICS at collection, no over-collection). ([PCPD framework](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/english/news_events/media_statements/press_20240611.html), [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2024/06/hong-kong-pcpd-issues-model-personal-data-protection-ai-framework), [DPO guideline](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202504/15/P2025041500227.htm), [PCPD mobile apps](https://www.pcpd.org.hk/mobileapps/practice.html))
- HK medical devices: MDACS is still **voluntary**; TR-007 (SaMD) and TR-008 (AI-MD, revised Oct 2025) apply only if you claim a medical purpose; mandatory regime (CMPR) expected 2026–27. No HK chatbot-specific law. ([NordPacific](https://nordpacificmed.com/hong-kong-revision-of-technical-references-artificial-intelligence-medical-devices-and-personalised-medical-devices-and-guidance-notes-definitions-and-ab/), [PharmaLex](https://www.pharmalex.com/thought-leadership/blogs/hong-kong-paves-the-way-for-mandatory-regulatory-framework-for-medical-devices/))
- HK crisis lines: Samaritans (multilingual, 24h) **2896 0000**; Suicide Prevention Services **2382 0000**; Samaritan Befrienders HK **2389 2222**; **Open Up** 24/7 text (WhatsApp/SMS 9101 2012, openup.hk; ages 6–24 focus); government **18111** Mental Health Support Hotline. ([Find a Helpline HK](https://findahelpline.com/countries/hk), [Open Up](https://www.openup.hk/en-us/service), [18111](https://www.shallwetalk.hk/en/get-help/mental-health-support-hotline-18111/))
- **Singapore**: HSA excludes software "intended solely for well-being or lifestyle" from device regulation *if* labeling/ads state it is not for medical use; MOH/HSA **AIHGle 2.0** (Mar 2026) applies to healthcare licensees; PDPC's 2024 Advisory Guidelines on AI recommendation/decision systems set expectations for consent, transparency and accountability; IMDA Model AI Governance Framework for GenAI is voluntary. ([HSA digital health](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/medical-devices/digital-health/), [Baker McKenzie AIHGle 2.0](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/03/singapore-moh-and-hsa-launch-refreshed-ai-in-healthcare-guidelines), [PDPC guidelines](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/guidelines-and-consultation/2024/02/advisory-guidelines-on-use-of-personal-data-in-ai-recommendation-and-decision-systems))

## 5. Platform & LLM-provider policies
- **Apple**: 1.4.1 — apps "should remind users to check with a doctor… before making medical decisions"; regulatory clearance link if a medical app; 1.4 — apps that "could be used for diagnosing or treating patients" get greater scrutiny; **5.1.2(i)** (enforced from 13 Nov 2025) — must "clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission"; 5.1.3 — no use of health data for advertising/data-mining, no PHI in iCloud; 5.1.1(ix) — healthcare apps must be submitted by a legal entity. ([Apple guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-apple-app-review-guidelines-ai-data-sharing/))
- **Google Play Health Content and Services**: mandatory Health apps declaration (all apps); non-device apps need an in-app disclaimer "not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent"; must remind users to consult a professional; no misleading/harmful health functionality; verified Organization Account for health apps from 28 Jan 2026. Separate **AI-Generated Content** policy requires in-app AI disclosure, in-app flag/report of offensive output, and preventing prohibited content generation. ([Health policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16679511?hl=en), [Health declaration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14738291?hl=en), [AI content policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14094294?hl=en))
- **Anthropic Usage Policy** (eff. 15 Sep 2025): "therapy, mental health" is a **High-Risk Use Case** requiring qualified-professional review of consumer-facing advice and AI disclosure at session start; but "**Wellness advice (e.g., advice on sleep, stress, nutrition, exercise, etc.) does not fall under this category**." All consumer chatbots must disclose AI at the start of each session. ([Anthropic AUP](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup))
- **OpenAI Usage Policies** (updated 29 Oct 2025): no "provision of tailored advice that requires a license, such as… medical advice, without appropriate involvement by a licensed professional"; no facilitation of suicide/self-harm; products are "not substitutes for professional medical… advice." ([OpenAI policies](https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies/), [Hooper Lundy](https://hooperlundy.com/openai-restricts-use-of-chatgpt-for-medical-advice/))

## 6. Clinical safety evidence

**Efficacy**
- Therabot RCT (NEJM AI, Mar 2025; n=210, 4 weeks vs waitlist): significant reductions in depression, GAD symptoms and eating-disorder risk; alliance ratings comparable to human therapy; authors stress clinician supervision. ([NEJM AI](https://ai.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/AIoa2400802), [Dartmouth](https://home.dartmouth.edu/news/2025/03/first-therapy-chatbot-trial-yields-mental-health-benefits))
- Woebot 2017 RCT (n=70, 2 weeks): depression reduced vs. NIMH ebook; anxiety improved in both arms. ([JMIR MH](https://mental.jmir.org/2017/2/e19/))
- Wysa: NHS HRA-registered clinical investigation; peer-reviewed chronic-pain/anxiety trial. ([HRA](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/planning-and-improving-research/application-summaries/research-summaries/clinical-investigation-of-wysa-v10/))
- 2026 meta-analyses: 48 RCTs/28,071 participants — anxiety SMD −0.20; another (34 studies) g=0.28 for anxiety; effects larger in clinical samples and shorter interventions. ([npj Digital Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02820-1), [npj Digital Medicine #2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02566-w))
- Single-session interventions: umbrella review of 24 systematic reviews/415 trials — 83% report positive effects incl. anxiety; online SSIs work at scale (n=2,452 RCT). ([Annual Review](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-clinpsy-081423-025033), [Nature Human Behaviour](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-021-01235-0))
- Unguided iCBT for anxiety is effective but smaller than guided; guided formats matter more for moderate–severe cases. ([JMIR unguided iCBT RCT](https://pediatrics.jmir.org/2024/1/e55786), [154-RCT durability meta-analysis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0272735824001399))

**Harms**
- Stanford (Moore et al., FAccT 2025): LLMs show stigma and "encourage clients' delusional thinking, likely due to their sycophancy"; failed on suicide-method prompts; bigger/newer models didn't fix it. ([ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3715275.3732039), [Stanford Report](https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2025/06/ai-mental-health-care-tools-dangers-risks))
- RAND/Psychiatric Services 2025: chatbots consistent at very-low/very-high suicide risk but inconsistent at intermediate levels. Scientific Reports 2025: 0 of 29 mental-health chatbot agents gave adequate crisis responses to C-SSRS-based escalation prompts. ([RAND](https://www.rand.org/news/press/2025/08/ai-chatbots-inconsistent-in-answering-questions-about.html), [Sci Rep](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-17242-4))
- OpenAI rolled back a sycophantic GPT-4o update (Apr 2025) that was "validating doubts, fueling anger, urging impulsive actions"; Oct 2025 update with 170+ clinicians targeting psychosis/mania, self-harm and emotional reliance; 2026 cross-conversation risk tracking. Case series describe "AI psychosis"/delusional spirals. ([OpenAI sycophancy](https://openai.com/index/sycophancy-in-gpt-4o/), [OpenAI sensitive conversations](https://openai.com/index/strengthening-chatgpt-responses-in-sensitive-conversations/), [JMIR MH](https://mental.jmir.org/2025/1/e85799))
- Litigation: *Raine v. OpenAI* (teen suicide, method guidance; active in CA); Character.AI/Google settled five teen-suicide/self-harm suits in Jan 2026; seven more suits vs OpenAI filed Nov 2025. ([ABA](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/health_law/news/2025/ai-chatbot-lawsuits-teen-mental-health/), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/07/openai-chatgpt-lawsuits-safety), [AEI](https://www.aei.org/technology-and-innovation/suicides-settlements-and-unresolved-chatbot-issues-a-long-litigation-road-lies-ahead/))

**Standard crisis approach**
- **C-SSRS Screener** (6 items: wish to be dead → ideation → method → intent → plan → behaviour) is public-domain, validated for lay administration, with triage bands (e.g., ideation with method/intent/plan or recent behaviour = high risk → immediate referral). ([Columbia Lighthouse](https://cssrs.columbia.edu/documents/frequent-screener/), [SPRC](https://sprc.org/resources/c-ssrs-columbia-suicide-severity-rating-scale-screener-training/))
- US: **988** call/text/chat; UK: Samaritans 116 123, Shout 85258, 111 opt 2; HK: 2896 0000 / 2382 0000 / Open Up 9101 2012 / 18111. ([988 Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/))

## 7. Toolkit intervention evidence
- **Physiological sigh / cyclic sighing**: Balban et al. 2023 (Cell Rep Med; RCT, 5 min/day × 1 month) — cyclic sighing beat mindfulness on mood and respiratory rate; 2026 EMA pilot: one-minute cyclic sighing and box breathing both reduced state anxiety vs control, more so in high-trait-anxiety users. ([Cell Rep Med](https://www.cell.com/cell-reports-medecine/fulltext/S2666-3791(22)00474-8), [Anxiety, Stress & Coping 2026](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10615806.2026.2659809))
- **Box breathing**: same two sources (an arm in Balban; equal to cyclic sighing acutely in the 2026 pilot).
- **5-4-3-2-1 grounding**: no dedicated RCT located; it is a sensory-grounding component of trauma-focused/CBT protocols with supportive small studies only — label as a coping skill, not a treatment. ([Thriving Center overview](https://thrivingcenterofpsych.com/blog/5-4-3-2-1-grounding-the-complete-technique-how-it-works-and-when-to-use-it/))
- **Cognitive restructuring**: CBT for anxiety disorders g=0.56 vs placebo (41 placebo-controlled RCTs). ([Carpenter 2018](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/da.22728))
- **Cognitive defusion**: randomized technique-evaluation trial (vocalizing strategies) — large reductions in distress vs waitlist; defusion ≥ restructuring for resisting cravings. ([Larsson et al.](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265010195_Cognitive_Defusion_for_Psychological_Distress_Dysphoria_and_Low_Self-Esteem_A_Randomized_Technique_Evaluation_Trial_of_Vocalizing_Strategies), [PubMed 22691136](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22691136/))
- **Behavioural activation**: 28-trial meta-analysis — g=0.37 on anxiety; digital BA also effective (JMIR 2025). ([Psychol Med 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32138802/), [JMIR 2025](https://www.jmir.org/2025/1/e68054))
- **Worry postponement**: effective in non-clinical samples (meta-analysis) and a 2024 two-session metacognitive WP trial reduced worry and negative metacognitions; evidence in diagnosed GAD is weaker. ([Clin Psychol Eur 2024](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11303915/), [WP meta-analysis](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/375654587_Effects_of_Worry_Postponement_on_Daily_Worry_a_Meta-Analysis))
- **Sleep/CBT-I components**: dCBT-I meta-analysis SMD −0.29 on anxiety; Sleepio IPD analysis — sleep improvement mediates 84% of anxiety effect, doubled odds of anxiety remission. ([PMC meta-analysis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10039857/), [J Affect Disord](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165032723008194))
- **Panic**: interoceptive exposure is the active ingredient; IE-inclusive CBT outperforms protocols without it (2025 RCT of exercise-based IE vs relaxation). Caution: breathing exercises used *to avoid* sensations can become safety behaviours. ([Front Psychiatry 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12926410/))

## Design implications
1. **Position and word it as wellness/self-help, everywhere.** No "therapy," "treat," "diagnose," "GAD/panic disorder," "clinically proven"; use "stress," "worry," "calm," "coping skills," "learn CBT-informed techniques." This keeps you outside FDA/MDR/MHRA SaMD, inside the Illinois/Nevada self-help exemption, inside Anthropic's wellness carve-out, and satisfies Google Play's disclaimer. Run marketing copy through a claims checklist before release.
2. **Never present the assistant as a therapist/clinician** (Tennessee, Nebraska/Idaho, GUARD Act). Give it a non-human name/persona; block user prompts asking it to "be my therapist" with a scripted redirect.
3. **AI disclosure at every session start, on return after >7 days, every 3 hours in long sessions, and on request** (Utah, NY, CA, EU Art. 50, Anthropic/OpenAI, Google Play). Log each disclosure event.
4. **Crisis flow, deterministic not LLM-only**: classifier + keyword layer runs on every user message; positive → C-SSRS-screener-style 2–6 structured questions in-app → tiered response (ideation-only: hotline card + safety-plan tool; method/intent/plan or recent behaviour: interrupt chat, full-screen crisis resources, one-tap call/text, encourage contacting emergency services). Locale-aware resource table (988 / 116 123 & 85258 & 111-2 / 2896 0000, 2382 0000, Open Up 9101 2012, 18111). Publish the protocol (CA SB 243) and record incidents (Oregon).
5. **Hard system-prompt rules against sycophancy**: no agreeing with catastrophic/delusional beliefs, no method information, no validating avoidance or self-harm; when a user states a distorted belief, use Socratic/defusion moves, not affirmation. Red-team with the Stanford/RAND/Sci-Rep prompt sets before each model change.
6. **No standalone "advice"**: the model guides exercises and reflection; personalised medical/medication questions get a "talk to a clinician" redirect (OpenAI/Anthropic licensing clauses, Apple 1.4.1, Google Play reminder).
7. **Age gating 18+** (or verified 13–17 with parental consent, break reminders, restricted content — CA SB 243, GUARD Act); collect DOB at signup and enforce in store listings.
8. **Explicit, separate health-data consent** (GDPR Art. 9, UK GDPR, WA MHMDA): a distinct screen naming "information about your mood, anxiety and sleep"; separate toggle for "learn my anxiety patterns"; no pre-ticked boxes; withdrawal = delete inferred profile.
9. **Zero advertising SDKs / no third-party analytics on chat content**; never sell or share health data (BetterHelp/Cerebral, HBNR, Apple 5.1.3, Utah, WA "sale" authorization). Use first-party, event-level analytics that never carry message text.
10. **Disclose and get explicit permission for sending data to the LLM provider** (Apple 5.1.2(i)); use zero-retention/no-training API terms; DPA/BAA-equivalent in place; document sub-processors in the privacy policy.
11. **Data minimisation and retention**: store chat transcripts encrypted, user-deletable, with default auto-expiry (e.g., 90 days) except crisis-event records kept longer (regulatory/reporting and litigation-hold); export and delete in-app; no PHI in iCloud (Apple 5.1.3(ii)).
12. **DPIA + PCPD/PDPC-style AI risk assessment** before launch; keep a model card, evaluation results, and a change log — needed for EU Art. 50 compliance, UK ICO, and as evidence in any FTC/PRA action.
13. **Model-change governance**: pin model versions; re-run safety evals (crisis, sycophancy, stigma) and a clinical review sign-off on every provider model upgrade (DHAC "drift" concern; Google Play prohibited-output duty).
14. **Clinical advisory sign-off** on all exercise scripts and safety rules (Anthropic high-risk expectation, DTAC clinical-safety case if NHS later); name a clinical safety lead.
15. **Exercise framing per evidence**: breathing = acute down-regulation (Balban); worry postponement = daily-worry tool, not GAD treatment; 5-4-3-2-1 = coping skill; for panic content, teach sensation-tolerance/interoceptive language and avoid framing breathing as "stopping" panic (safety-behaviour risk).
16. **Escalation off-ramps to humans**: always-visible "Talk to a person" button (hotlines, find-a-therapist links, NHS 111/GP, HK Open Up); message when usage patterns suggest reliance (e.g., >X sessions/day) — anti-emotional-dependence measure (OpenAI/FTC concerns, EU Art. 5 vulnerability).
17. **In-app report/flag button on every AI message** and a takedown/feedback loop (Google Play AI content policy).
18. **Geo-aware compliance switches**: state/country config for disclosure cadence, minors rules, crisis resources, and the option to disable the chat in jurisdictions where "AI therapy" bans are interpreted broadly (IL, NV, ME, RI, CO) if counsel advises.
19. **Store metadata**: complete Google Play Health apps declaration (non-device, wellness), verified Organization account; Apple submission by legal entity, privacy nutrition labels marking Health & Fitness/sensitive data.
20. **Evidence and outcome tracking**: use GAD-7/GAD-2 only as self-monitoring (not diagnosis, with wording "not a diagnosis"); if you later run outcome research, IRB/ethics approval and research consent (Apple 5.1.3(iii–iv)).
