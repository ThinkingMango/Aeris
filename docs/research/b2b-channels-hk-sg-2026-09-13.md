# B2B & Institutional Channels for a HK-founded Wellness Anxiety App

*Research memo compiled 13 Sep 2026. Product context: AI conversation + short exercises + pattern insights; explicitly "not therapy, not a medical device". Summarised and applied in `../04-viable-options.md`.*

**Framing caveat up front:** "not therapy, not a medical device" is a strength for speed (no regulatory path) and a weakness for every channel below that is run by insurers, HR benefits teams, universities or clinicians, all of whom increasingly want either clinical validation, an EAP with human counsellors behind the app, or both. Treat the AI-conversation feature as a liability in procurement conversations unless you can show a documented safety/escalation layer.

---

## 1. Employer mental-health market: Hong Kong and Singapore

### Prevalence (the demand side is not the problem)

- **Hong Kong:** Mind HK × Manulife (2025) estimate 46% of Hongkongers have mild-to-severe symptoms of depression, anxiety or both; 16%+ in the moderate-severe range; 18–29s worst affected ([Mind HK press release](https://www.mind.org.hk/new/press-release-nearly-half-of-hongkongers-show-signs-of-depression-anxiety-or-both-co-study-by-mind-hk-and-manulife-hong-kong-estimates/)). AXA Mind Health 2025 (fieldwork Oct–Nov 2024, n=1,000 HK): 60% report work-related stress vs 56% global; 23% took sick leave for mind-health reasons (40% of Gen Z); **29% already use mind-health apps (46% of Gen Z)** ([AXA / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/axa-study-over-60-of-hong-kong-workers-encounter-elevated-stress-levels-exceeding-global-average-302462998.html)). Cigna International Health Study 2025: 77% of Hongkongers report stress ([Cigna HK](https://www.cigna.com.hk/en/about-us/corporate-news/2025/cigna-healthcare-international-health-study-2025)).
- **Singapore:** TELUS Mental Health Index (5 quarterly surveys, 2024–early 2025): 40% face constant stress, >1/3 at high mental-health risk, 39% say mental health hurts productivity (48% of Gen Z), and **52% say their workplace provides no mental-health programme** ([TELUS / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/telus-mental-health-index-singapores-workforce-faces-growing-mental-health-crisis-threatening-productivity-and-physical-health-302312903.html); [TELUS SG barometer](https://resources.telushealth.com/en/mhi-barometer-sg)). Employment Hero 2024: 61% of SG employees report burnout ([Mavenside summary](https://www.mavenside.co/blog/workplace-mental-health-singapore-2026-employer-guide)).

### EAP penetration

- **HK:** MixCare 2026 survey of 107 HR leaders: ~54–55% of employers provide EAP/mental-health support; 49% want to improve it ([MixCare](https://m.mixcarehealth.com/articles/2026-hk-benefits-trends.html)). Howden's HK Employee Benefits Report 2025 is the broker-side reference ([Howden](https://www.howdengroup.com/hk-en/hong-kong-employee-benefits-report-2025)). CIPD/AIA Asia Employee Benefits Report 2025 surveyed ~117 HK and ~116 SG employers ([CIPD PDF](https://www.cipd.org/globalassets/media/knowledge/knowledge-hub/reports/2025-pdfs/8892-employee-benefits-asia-report-web.pdf)); the EAP line was not extracted from the PDF; read it before pitching.
- **SG:** Aon 2025 Employee Benefits Survey (200+ orgs, Apr–May 2025): 72% of employers offer EAP; "mental health is not a common insured benefit" ([Aon](https://www.aon.com/apac/insights/human-capital-solutions/2025-aon-employee-benefits-survey-singapore-survey-highlights)). Mental health becomes a protected characteristic under the Workplace Fairness Act (enforcement 2026–27, employers 25+) ([Mavenside](https://www.mavenside.co/blog/workplace-mental-health-singapore-2026-employer-guide)); the 2023 Tripartite Advisory nudges but doesn't mandate EAP ([MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/tripartism-in-singapore/tripartite-guidelines-and-advisories/tripartite-advisory-on-mental-well-being-at-workplaces)).

**Implication:** in SG the EAP slot is mostly filled; you are a displacement or add-on sale. In HK roughly half of employers have nothing, but those are disproportionately SMEs with no benefits budget.

### What employers pay

- **HK:** HK$1,500–2,500 per employee per year is the only published HK range found, and it comes from a vendor marketing page, so treat as indicative ([MantraCare](https://mantracare.org/employee-wellness/eap-guide/eap-hong-kong/)).
- **SG:** no published per-employee benchmark. Pay-per-use proxies: Safe Space charges ~S$80 (video) / S$120 (in-person) per session and sells "pay-per-use EAP" to employers ([Vulcan Post](https://vulcanpost.com/712908/safe-space-mental-health-online-counselling/); [Safe Space business page](https://www.safespace.sg/business/)).
- **Global digital PEPM:** US$2–12 PEPM for wellness apps ([Taction](https://www.tactionsoft.com/blog/corporate-wellness-app-cost/)); Lyra/Spring-type clinical platforms US$4–18 PEPM; health-system/insurer clinical grade US$50–100 PMPM ([ResearchAndMarkets summary](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/report/digital-mental-health)). Headspace explains its two models (flat PEPM vs low base + utilisation fees) without disclosing numbers ([Headspace](https://organizations.headspace.com/blog/evaluating-mental-health-solution-pricing-models)).

## 2. Insurer wellness channels (HK/SG)

- **AIA:** AIA Vitality HK partner list has *no* mental-wellbeing app partner at all; partners are gyms, cinemas, Garmin, dietitians ([AIA HK partners](https://www.aia.com.hk/en/health-and-wellness/aia-vitality/our-partners)). AIA SG's "Think Well" mental-health benefit for corporate policyholders runs through WhiteCoat telehealth, and from 2025 inpatient mental care is auto-included in group plans ([AIA SG](https://www.aia.com.sg/en/about-aia/media-centre/press-releases/2024/aia-2025-group-insurance-enhancements)). ThoughtFull cites a 43% utilisation figure on an AIA deployment ([ThoughtFull](https://www.thoughtfull.world/solutions/eap-singapore)).
- **Manulife HK:** two-year strategic charity partnership with Mind HK (from May 2024) funding iACT practitioners and a Mong Kok consultation hub; a CSR/NGO play, not an app bundle ([Manulife](https://www.manulife.com.hk/en/individual/about/newsroom/manulife-partners-with-mind-hk-to-establish-new-wellbeing-consultation-hub-in-mong-kok.html)).
- **FWD:** bundled ThoughtFull's "Mind Strength Support" for HK and Thailand customers (2022) ([PR Newswire](https://en.prnasia.com/releases/apac/digital-mental-health-company-thoughtfull-partners-with-fwd-to-provide-science-backed-and-technology-enabled-mental-health-solutions-for-customers-in-hong-kong-and-thailand-387735.shtml)).
- **MSIG HK:** gave SME group-medical policyholders free Intellect access as a time-boxed promotion (Jul–Dec 2022) ([MSIG](https://www.msig.com.hk/en/corporate-news/msig-hong-kong-engages-intellect-provide-holistic-mental-health-support)).
- **Prudential:** Talk It Out with Intellect (Jul 2025), Malaysia/Vietnam, foundation-funded ([Prudential plc](https://www.prudentialplc.com/en/newsroom/company-news/2025/prudential-and-prudence-foundation-provide-free-mental-health-support-to-vulnerable-communities/)).
- **Bupa HK:** "Supporting Healthier Minds" and MindCare recognition scheme; content co-created with KELY/Coolminds, not a third-party app ([Bupa](https://www.bupa.com.hk/en/companies-employees/supporting-healthier-minds/); [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/bupa-mental-health-campaign-hong-kong)).
- **AXA HK:** partnered with Oxford VR + CUHK on VR treatment for corporate customers ([Business Chief](https://businesschief.asia/human-capital/the-apps-tackling-asias-workplace-mental-health-crisis)).
- **Cigna:** global Headspace collaboration (Nov 2025), not HK-specific ([MedCity](https://medcitynews.com/2025/11/cigna-headspace-mental-health/)).

**How vendors get in:** every published example is either (i) a scaled vendor with human clinicians behind the app (Intellect, ThoughtFull), (ii) an NGO/charity, or (iii) a university research partner. No insurer has publicly bundled a self-help-only AI app. Insurer deals are 12–18-month, multi-stakeholder procurements. **Not a solo-founder channel in 6–12 months**, except possibly a small time-boxed promo with a second-tier insurer's SME line (the MSIG model).

## 3. Universities and schools

**Capacity/wait times:** HKU CEDARS routine wait is 2–4 weeks ([HKU CEDARS FAQ](https://www.cedars.hku.hk/cope/cps/faqs)); NUS UCS historically 3–8 weeks before a service redesign ([NUS](https://www.nus.edu.sg/inside-nus/stories/transformation-of-university-counselling-services-to-support-wellness-of-the-community)). CUHK non-local students capped at 6 sessions ([CUHK OSA](https://www.osa.cuhk.edu.hk/newsletterdetails/mental-health-support-for-new-students/)).

**Prevalence:** HK university students 2016–2021: 68.5% with depressive symptoms, 49.4% at-risk, rising to 80%/60% by 2021 ([Int J Adolescence & Youth, 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02673843.2025.2496445)); moderate+ anxiety ~50% during COVID ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9277093/)). HKFYG 2025: 27.8% of secondary students report anxiety, a five-year high ([HKFYG](https://hkfyg.org.hk/en/2025/10/14/%E9%9D%92%E5%8D%94%E5%85%AC%E5%B8%83%E3%80%8C%E4%B8%AD%E5%AD%B8%E7%94%9F%E9%96%8B%E5%AD%B8%E9%81%A9%E6%87%89%E8%AA%BF%E6%9F%A5%E3%80%8D%E7%B5%90%E6%9E%9C-2/)); HKCSS: 40% of DSE students show depression/anxiety symptoms ([HKCSS](https://www.hkcss.org.hk/%E5%9B%9B%E6%88%90%E5%8F%97%E8%A8%AAhkdse%E5%AD%B8%E7%94%9F%E5%91%88%E7%8F%BE%E6%8A%91%E9%AC%B1%E6%88%96%E7%84%A6%E6%85%AE%E7%97%87%E7%8B%80%EF%BC%8C%E6%B1%82%E5%8A%A9%E6%84%8F%E9%A1%98%E4%BD%8E/?lang=en)). SG National Youth Mental Health Study (n=2,600, 15–35): 30.6% severe/extremely severe depression, anxiety or stress; anxiety 27% ([Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2024/09/youth-mental-health-study-imh/); [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1876201825000486)).

**What's been procured:** CUHK's Jockey Club TourHeart+ launched a GenAI chatbot in April 2024, wholly funded by HKJC Charities Trust since 2017 ([SCMP, Jan 2025](https://www.scmp.com/special-reports/article/3293113/how-hong-kong-universities-are-tackling-rising-mental-health-challenges-among-students-help-ai); [Alliance Magazine](https://www.alliancemagazine.org/blog/how-philathropy-funded-ai-therapy-is-supporting-hong-kongs-gen-z/)). NUS uses Intellect (RCT-backed; 44.8% of students on the anxiety path saw significant reduction) ([NUS](https://www.nus.edu.sg/hwb/students/)). NTU ran an AI-powered psychological first aid campaign ([NTU](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/news/detail/ntu-sets-national-record-with-ai-powered-psychological-first-aid-campaign)). Singapore's national mindline.sg embeds Wysa's chatbot under a national subscription ([Wysa](https://blogs.wysa.io/blog/covid/new-stress-and-coping-tool-features-wysa-to-help-singaporeans-in-a-post-pandemic-world-adjust-to-life-in-a-new-normal); [MOHT](https://www.moht.com.sg/our-programmes/mindline-sg/)). HK EDB regularised the three-tier school emergency mechanism in 2025/26 and runs Mental Health@School ([LCQ21, May 2025](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202505/21/P2025052100581.htm)).

**Honest read:** HK universities build in-house with HKJC money or with their own psychology departments; SG universities buy Intellect or use the national platform. A solo founder's realistic entry is a **student society / residential college pilot** (free, 1 semester, PHQ-9/GAD-7 pre-post), not a procurement. Secondary schools require EDB-aligned safeguarding and are slower still.

## 4. Therapist-adjunct / between-session tools

- **Wysa Copilot** (Nov 2024): clinicians assign CBT tools and monitor completion; selected by NHS Talking Therapies; users 3× likelier to complete therapy with between-session AI support ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241105716965/en/Wysa-Unveils-Wysa-Copilot-to-Elevate-Mental-Health-Therapy-With-AI); [Wysa](https://www.wysa.com/wysa-copilot)). Consumer premium US$74.99/yr; clinician pricing not published ([ChoosingTherapy](https://www.choosingtherapy.com/wysa-app-review/)).
- **Limbic Access:** UK G-Cloud price card: from £5.49 per licence, £35,000 minimum, 2-year fixed contracts ([G-Cloud pricing PDF](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/719815/270128099572649-pricing-document-2024-05-06-1028.pdf)). Sold to services, not individual therapists.
- **SilverCloud (Amwell):** platform licence priced per capita on referrals; figures not disclosed ([G-Cloud](https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/documents/719765/767278460509582-pricing-document-2024-05-06-1610.pdf)).
- **Kintsugi:** voice-biomarker API embedded in telehealth products; pricing gated ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/kintsugi/pricing)). **Rosebud:** consumer journaling at US$9.99/mo, no real therapist tier ([Agent Finder](https://agent-finder.co/reviews/rosebud)).

**HK private psychology market:** ~8.2 clinical psychologists per 100k population vs 28 UK / 33.9 US ([Healthy Matters](https://www.healthymatters.com.hk/mental-health-in-hong-kong-seeing-a-psychologist/)); the DCP private-practice list is the sales list ([HKPS-DCP 2025 PDF](https://hkps-dcp.org.hk/images/downloads/EPP%20List%20-%202025.pdf)). Fees: HK$1,600–2,600 per 50-min session at group practices; HK$1,500–3,500/hr for clinical psychologists ([TherapyRoute 2025](https://www.therapyroute.com/article/how-much-does-therapy-cost-in-hongkong-complete-2025-guide); [Central Minds](https://www.centralminds.hk/faq/what-are-your-fees-per-session/)). No published market-size figure exists; the private-practice pool is a few hundred practitioners, so this is a **small, relationship-sold niche**, not a market.

**Honest read:** every scaled between-session product is sold to a *service* (NHS, HSE, health system) with a clinical-safety file. Individual HK/SG therapists can be signed one at a time on a per-clinician plan (e.g., HK$300–500/month for 10 client seats) but it is high-touch and clinicians will ask for supervision-grade transcripts and risk flags.

## 5. Licensing the safety/conversation layer as an API

- **Clare&me** (Berlin) pivoted from consumer app to "Conversational APIs for behavioral health" / clinical LLM API after a €3.7M round in Aug 2024, and **filed for bankruptcy in June 2025** ([clareandme.com](https://www.clareandme.com/); [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2024/08/berlin-based-clareme-raises-e3-7-million-to-train-an-empathic-clinical-llm/); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/491513-95)). The pivot did not save it.
- **Limbic Layer:** a proprietary safety/clinical-reasoning layer between users and the LLM; Nature Medicine paper (2026) claims 43% higher therapeutic competence than foundation models, but Limbic keeps it inside its own regulated products rather than licensing it broadly ([Limbic](https://www.limbic.ai/layer); [Nature Medicine](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04278-w)).
- **Wysa:** licensed its chatbot to Singapore's mindline.sg (national subscription), the strongest APAC "engine licensing" precedent, but Wysa had years of evidence and ISO 13485 first ([Wysa/mindline](https://blogs.wysa.io/blog/covid/new-stress-and-coping-tool-features-wysa-to-help-singaporeans-in-a-post-pandemic-world-adjust-to-life-in-a-new-normal)).
- **Demand signals:** NY (Nov 2025) and California (Jan 2026) now mandate self-harm detection and AI disclosure for companion bots ([Kyan Health](https://www.kyanhealth.com/post/ai-mental-health-tools-in-the-workplace-the-safety-questions-to-ask-in-2026)); Singapore MOH's AI in Healthcare Guidelines 2.0 stress augmentation of professionals ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/others/health-regulation/emerging-regulatory-policy-issues/)); practitioners are publicly calling for a "missing safety layer" ([Wallace, Substack](https://drscottwallace.substack.com/p/the-missing-safety-layer-for-mental)). No public pricing exists for any of these APIs.

**Honest read:** the need is real and growing, but buyers (HR platforms, wellness apps) will want a validated crisis-detection benchmark, not a wrapper around a prompt. A single-founder company can sell this only as **consulting-plus-code to 1–3 friendly HK/SG wellness or HR startups**. It's a side revenue line and a credibility asset, not a channel.

## 6. Government and NGO channels

**Hong Kong**
- *Shall We Talk* (DH + Advisory Committee on Mental Health): a promotion brand, reached 45,560 students via 2025 roadshows; it links to resources but doesn't procure apps ([LCQ12, Oct 2025](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202510/22/P2025102200585.htm); [Shall We Talk links](https://www.shallwetalk.hk/en/resources/related-links/)).

HKJC Charities Trust is the real "digital mental health procurer" in HK, and it funds NGOs/universities, not startups.

- *Mental Health Initiatives Funding Scheme*: HK$300M earmarked, later reported HK$475M across 172 projects; eligible applicants are SWD-subvented NGOs, HKCSS members and post-secondary institutions; **startups must partner with an NGO** ([Health Bureau](https://www.healthbureau.gov.hk/en/fund/210700_mhifs/background.html); [EASAP 2025](https://www.easap.asia/index.php/find-issues/current-issue/item/1005-2025-v35n2-p109)).

**Singapore**
- *mindline.sg* (MOHT, with IMH/MSF/NCSS) is the national front door and already embeds Wysa; the national helpline/textline went live mid-2025 ([MOHT](https://www.moht.com.sg/our-programmes/mindline-sg/); [JMIR 2024](https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e44443)). A new vendor would need to displace Wysa; not plausible.
- *Grants*: Startup SG Founder S$20–50K at 1:1 matching via an Accredited Mentor Partner (post-Apr 2024) ([grants.sg](https://grants.sg/grants/startup-sg-founder)); Startup SG Tech POC/POV ceilings raised in 2025 ([DeepTechSG](https://www.deeptech.sg/startup-sg-tech-grant-increase-2025-poc-pov/)); requires SG entity.

## 7. B2B2C unit economics

- **PEPM:** US$2–12 for wellness apps; US$4–18 for Lyra/Spring-class; US$50–100 PMPM for clinical-grade insurer contracts (sources in §1).
- **Utilisation:** 85% of employers with an EAP report 3–5% take-up; 27% of employees don't know it exists ([Personnel Today, 2024](https://www.personneltoday.com/hr/take-up-of-eaps-low-research-2024/)); US median ~5.5%, UK 10.4% ([Meditopia](https://meditopia.com/en/forwork/articles/eap-statistics-and-utilization-rates); [Taylor Benefits](https://www.taylorbenefitsinsurance.com/eap-utilization-rates-are-at-5-fix-your-program-before-the-next-renewal/)). Digital-first vendors claim 20–35% sign-up in year one (Spring Health 20%+, Intellect 35%, ThoughtFull 43–69% on named accounts), but "signed up" ≠ monthly active ([Spring Health](https://www.springhealth.com/blog/how-innovative-eaps-drive-utilization); [Intellect](https://intellect.co/); [ThoughtFull](https://www.thoughtfull.world/solutions/eap-singapore)). Insignia's write-up of Intellect: 20–45% engagement within 3 months at 5,000+ employee accounts vs 1–2% for legacy EAP ([Insignia](https://review.insignia.vc/2025/03/21/intellect-case-study/)).
- **Sales cycles:** B2B SaaS median 84 days; healthcare-adjacent 12–14.7 months with ~9 decision-makers ([Boomerang](https://getboomerang.ai/glossaries/b2b-sales-cycle-benchmarks-2026); [Sagefrog](https://www.sagefrog.com/blog/b2b-healthcare-marketing-in-2026-how-to-win-longer-sales-cycles/)). Employer benefits in HK/SG renew annually via brokers; miss the window and you wait a year.
- **Pilots:** the norm is a 60–90-day, single-department pilot with pre-agreed metrics ([Martal](https://martal.ca/medical-software-sales-lb/)). At 200 employees × US$5 PEPM × 3 months, a pilot is worth ~US$3,000. Intellect's "35% sign-up in year one" is the bar an HR buyer will hold you to.

## Which channels are realistic for a solo founder in 6–12 months

| Channel | Realistic solo? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Large employer EAP replacement (HK/SG) | **No** | 72% SG penetration; Intellect/ThoughtFull with clinicians + AI; broker-gated annual cycles |
| Insurer bundling | **No** | Every precedent is a scaled clinical vendor or NGO; 12–18-month procurement |
| University procurement | **No** | HKJC-funded in-house (CUHK) or Intellect (NUS); mindline.sg locked to Wysa |
| Secondary schools | **No** | EDB safeguarding, three-tier mechanism, slow |
| Government funding (MHIFS, ITF) | **Only via NGO/university partner** | Eligibility excludes companies |
| SME employers (10–200 staff), card-paid | **Yes** | ~45% of HK employers offer nothing; no broker; owner decides |
| Individual private therapists/coaches | **Yes, small** | Few hundred HK practitioners; relationship sales; HK$1,600–2,600 sessions leave room for a HK$300–500/mo tool |
| NGO co-branding / Mind HK listing | **Yes** | Free, credibility, feeds MHIFS-eligible partnerships |
| Student society pilots | **Yes** | Zero-cost, generates GAD-7 pre/post evidence |
| Safety-layer API | **Side revenue at best** | Clare&me's pivot failed; no public pricing; buyers want validation |

## Ranked: the 5 most realistic channels

1. **HK SMEs and startups (20–200 staff) on a self-serve monthly plan (~HK$50–80/employee/month, no contract).** Evidence: ~45% of HK employers have no EAP/mental-health support ([MixCare 2026](https://m.mixcarehealth.com/articles/2026-hk-benefits-trends.html)); 60% of HK workers report work stress and 29% already use mind-health apps ([AXA 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/axa-study-over-60-of-hong-kong-workers-encounter-elevated-stress-levels-exceeding-global-average-302462998.html)); HK EAP pricing sits at HK$1,500–2,500/employee/year, which a cheap app undercuts ([MantraCare](https://mantracare.org/employee-wellness/eap-guide/eap-hong-kong/)); the incumbents chase "the largest employers and the largest insurers" ([Insignia](https://review.insignia.vc/2024/02/14/mental-health-care-singapore-intellect/)). Cyberport/HKSTP cohorts are a warm SME list ([Cyberport CCMF](https://www.cyberport.hk/en/entrepreneurship/cyberport_creative_micro_fund/)).

2. **Private therapists, counsellors and coaches in HK (then SG) as a between-session companion, per-clinician subscription.** Evidence: Wysa Copilot and Limbic show between-session AI is a validated category with 3× completion effects ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241105716965/en/Wysa-Unveils-Wysa-Copilot-to-Elevate-Mental-Health-Therapy-With-AI)); HK has 8.2 CPs per 100k and 1–2-year public waits, so private practitioners are capacity-constrained ([Healthy Matters](https://www.healthymatters.com.hk/mental-health-in-hong-kong-seeing-a-psychologist/)); session fees of HK$1,600–2,600 make a HK$300–500/month tool trivially affordable ([TherapyRoute](https://www.therapyroute.com/article/how-much-does-therapy-cost-in-hongkong-complete-2025-guide)); the DCP private-practice list is public ([HKPS-DCP](https://hkps-dcp.org.hk/images/downloads/EPP%20List%20-%202025.pdf)). Caveat: needs risk-flagging and exportable summaries before clinicians will trust it.

3. **NGO co-branding and listing (Mind HK, KELY/Coolminds, HKFYG), leading to an MHIFS-eligible joint bid.** Evidence: MHIFS money (HK$300M+ earmarked; HK$475M/172 projects reported) flows only to NGOs and post-secondary institutions ([Health Bureau](https://www.healthbureau.gov.hk/en/fund/210700_mhifs/background.html)); HKJC's Open Up shows philanthropy pays for digital youth support at scale (SROI 4.74) ([HKJC](https://corporate.hkjc.com/en-US/news-and-publications/corporate-news/2026-03/news_2026031401400)); Bupa and Manulife route their mental-health CSR through Mind HK/KELY, so an NGO badge is the only realistic door to insurers ([Manulife](https://www.manulife.com.hk/en/individual/about/newsroom/manulife-partners-with-mind-hk-to-establish-new-wellbeing-consultation-hub-in-mong-kok.html); [Bupa](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/bupa-mental-health-campaign-hong-kong)).

4. **University student-society and residential-college pilots (HKU/CUHK/PolyU; NUS/NTU/SMU) to generate GAD-7 evidence.** Evidence: 27–50% of students at moderate+ anxiety ([IJAY 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02673843.2025.2496445); [IMH NYMHS](https://mothership.sg/2024/09/youth-mental-health-study-imh/)); HKU waits 2–4 weeks, NUS historically 3–8 ([HKU](https://www.cedars.hku.hk/cope/cps/faqs); [NUS](https://www.nus.edu.sg/inside-nus/stories/transformation-of-university-counselling-services-to-support-wellness-of-the-community)); SMU's psychology society already runs PHQ-9/GAD-7 events with mindline.sg ([SMU](https://news.smu.edu.sg/news/2025/10/30/smu-mental-health-week-2025)). This won't pay, but it produces the outcome data every other channel demands.

5. **Second-tier insurer SME promotions (MSIG model) and broker "wellness add-on" slots, only after 1–4 produce evidence.** Evidence: MSIG HK gave SME group-medical policyholders a free 6-month Intellect promo ([MSIG](https://www.msig.com.hk/en/corporate-news/msig-hong-kong-engages-intellect-provide-holistic-mental-health-support)); FWD bundled ThoughtFull in HK ([PR Newswire](https://en.prnasia.com/releases/apac/digital-mental-health-company-thoughtfull-partners-with-fwd-to-provide-science-backed-and-technology-enabled-mental-health-solutions-for-customers-in-hong-kong-and-thailand-387735.shtml)); AIA Vitality HK has an empty mental-wellbeing partner slot ([AIA](https://www.aia.com.hk/en/health-and-wellness/aia-vitality/our-partners)). Realistic only in months 9–18 and only as a free-to-insurer promo, not a paid bundle.

**Explicitly not in the top five:** Intellect-style enterprise EAP (needs a sales team, clinicians and an ISO 27001/13485 posture), mindline.sg/government platforms (Wysa incumbent), and selling the guardrail layer as an API (Clare&me pivoted to exactly this in 2024 and was bankrupt by June 2025).
